ABOUT · HOW I BUILD

Devices that explain themselves to the people using them.

I’m a B.Tech student in Computer Science and Engineering (IoT), 2023–2027, at Parul Institute of Engineering and Technology. My projects start with a physical signal and follow it all the way to a screen: reading sensors on a microcontroller, sending the data over Wi-Fi, storing it in a cloud database, and showing it in a web interface.

I like the unglamorous parts of that chain: wiring and calibrating sensors, handling serial and wireless communication, and working out where a prototype’s limits are by testing it, then writing those limits down.
